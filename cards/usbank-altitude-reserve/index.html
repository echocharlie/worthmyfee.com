Is the U.S. Bank Altitude Reserve worth its $400 annual fee?

Short answer: no combination of the U.S. Bank Altitude Reserve’s credits covers its $400 fee; all 2 together come to $350. The remaining $50 has to come from status, lounges, perks and points. The catch is where it pays: $325 of the $350 advertised only works through U.S. Bank Travel Center.

Traps and friction

Where the U.S. Bank Altitude Reserve’s credits leak.

Portal-only, but not hotel-only. $325 Travel Center Credit can be spent on flights or car rentals as well as hotels. If you hold hotel status, spend it on the flights or the car: a portal hotel booking makes the portal the merchant of record, and the chain will not honor your status or post points.
