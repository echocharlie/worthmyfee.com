Break-even is the share of the advertised credits you would have to reach for the credits alone to cover the fee. A card can still earn its keep through status, lounges and points; the audit values those against your own charges. Status trap counts credits that only pay on prepaid portal hotel bookings, which void elite recognition at the major chains.
