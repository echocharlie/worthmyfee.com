Is the American Express Delta SkyMiles Platinum Business worth its $350 annual fee?

Short answer: the Amex Delta SkyMiles Platinum Business’s $350 fee is covered by two credits alone, Annual Companion Certificate ($300) and Delta Stays Hotel Credit ($200), if you actually use them, and Delta Stays Hotel Credit voids hotel elite status on the stays it pays for. The catch is where it pays: $200 of the $770 advertised only works through Delta Stays, which voids hotel elite status on those stays.

What it takes to cover the $350 fee

To cover the $350 fee on credits alone you need to reach $350 of the $770 advertised, which is 46% of the headline number. How hard that is depends on when and where each credit pays.

Of the $770: $240 arrives in monthly, quarterly or half-year chunks that do not roll over, so those two credits need matching spend on schedule, every period, or the money is gone; $200 only pays on bookings made through Delta Stays; $240 pays nothing until you enroll or activate it; $300 is delivered off the card, as in-app credit, certificates or complimentary subscriptions, which is worth something only if you would have paid for those anyway.

$300 of the advertised value comes from terms the issuer states ambiguously; the catalog marks those unverified and the audit scores them conservatively.

What you actually reach depends on your own charges: the audit matches every credit against 12 months of real transactions, counts what actually landed on the card toward the fee, and shows the rest as upside.

What makes the Amex Delta SkyMiles Platinum Business’s credits hard to use

Portal hotel bookings void elite-status recognition. Delta Stays Hotel Credit ($200 a year) pays only on prepaid bookings through Delta Stays, which makes the portal, not the hotel, the merchant of record. Major hotel programs treat these as third-party bookings: elite benefits are not honored at check-in (upgrades, breakfast, late checkout), and the stay earns no points. If you hold hotel status, redeeming this credit typically costs more per stay than the credit returns. If you are actively requalifying for status, every stay routed through the portal to capture this credit is a night that doesn’t count — and a shortfall at year-end can cost an entire tier, worth far more than the credit.Program terms: marriott.com, hilton.com, ihg.com, world.hyatt.com.
  • Monthly credits. Resy Dining Credit and Rideshare Credit: $240 a year, but only if the matching charge lands every month. Five orders in a year on a monthly credit pays out for five months.
  • Enrollment required. Resy Dining Credit and Rideshare Credit pay nothing until enrolled or activated. The audit checks your statement for the issuer’s own credit postings, so an unenrolled credit shows up as exactly that.

Beyond the credits: status, lounges and points

  • Delta flight perks: a free checked bag worth $35 each way, for you and up to 8 companions, when the ticket is bought with the card. Counted on flights the card can see you take, and worth nothing once you hold Silver Medallion status, which includes them.
  • Delta SkyMiles status support: 2,500 qualifying dollars toward requalification, plus 1 per $20 spent on the card. Worth something only while you are actively chasing that status.

Questions about the Amex Delta SkyMiles Platinum Business’s credits

How much are the Amex Delta SkyMiles Platinum Business’s credits worth?

$770 a year at face value across five credits, against a $350 annual fee. Covering the fee on credits alone takes 46% of that. $240 arrives in monthly, quarterly or half-year chunks that expire unused. What you reach depends on your own charges; Worth My Fee scores each credit against 12 months of real transactions.

Which Amex Delta SkyMiles Platinum Business credits expire every month?

Resy Dining Credit ($10 a month, $120 a year) and Rideshare Credit ($10 a month, $120 a year). Each month’s amount is lost if unused, so the annual figure assumes a matching charge in all twelve months.

Does the Amex Delta SkyMiles Platinum Business’s Delta Stays Hotel Credit honor hotel elite status?

No. It pays only on prepaid bookings through Delta Stays, which makes the portal the merchant of record. Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt and IHG treat that as a third-party stay: no elite benefits at check-in, no points, and no qualifying nights toward status. If you hold hotel status, the credit typically costs more per stay than it returns.

Which Amex Delta SkyMiles Platinum Business credits require enrollment or activation?

Resy Dining Credit and Rideshare Credit. They pay nothing until switched on; the issuer’s own statement-credit postings show whether that has happened.

What is the downgrade option for the Amex Delta SkyMiles Platinum Business?

The American Express Delta SkyMiles Gold Business ($150 a year). Worth My Fee scores it on your same year of charges: if a cheaper card clears its fee the verdict is downgrade, and if none does the verdict is cancel.

What does the Amex Delta SkyMiles Platinum Business earn on spending?

3× on Delta purchases, 3× on hotels booked direct and 1.5× on transit, US shipping & large purchases, and 1× on everything else. Worth My Fee values Delta SkyMiles at 1.2¢ per point, and counts only the premium over a no-fee 2% cash-back card toward the fee.
