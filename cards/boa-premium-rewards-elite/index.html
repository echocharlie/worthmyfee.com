Is the Bank of America Premium Rewards Elite worth its $550 annual fee?

Short answer: no combination of the BofA Premium Rewards Elite’s credits covers its $550 fee; all 3 together come to $480. The remaining $70 has to come from status, lounges, perks and points. Most of it posts automatically; the question is whether you spend at these merchants at all.

$480 advertised. $550 to cover.

Even if you reached every one of the 3 credits, they would cover $480 of the $550 fee. The remaining $70 has to come from status, lounges, perks and the points the card earns, all valued below.
