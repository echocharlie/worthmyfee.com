Is the American Express Platinum Card for Morgan Stanley worth its $895 annual fee?

Short answer: the Amex Platinum Card for Morgan Stanley’s $895 fee is covered by two credits alone, Hotel Credit (FHR / The Hotel Collection) ($600) and Resy Dining Credit ($400), if you actually use them. The catch is cadence: $1,955 of the $3,024 advertised arrives in monthly, quarterly or half-year chunks that expire unused.

What it takes to cover the $895 fee

To cover the $895 fee on credits alone you need to reach $895 of the $3,024 advertised, which is 30% of the headline number. How hard that is depends on when and where each credit pays.

Of the $3,024: $1,955 arrives in monthly, quarterly or half-year chunks that do not roll over, so those six credits need matching spend on schedule, every period, or the money is gone; $600 only pays on bookings made through Amex Travel (Fine Hotels + Resorts / The Hotel Collection); $2,775 pays nothing until you enroll or activate it; $200 is delivered off the card, as in-app credit, certificates or complimentary subscriptions, which is worth something only if you would have paid for those anyway.

$3,024 of the advertised value comes from terms the issuer states ambiguously; the catalog marks those unverified and the audit scores them conservatively.

What you actually reach depends on your own charges: the audit matches every credit against 12 months of real transactions, counts what actually landed on the card toward the fee, and shows the rest as upside.

Questions about the Amex Platinum Card for Morgan Stanley’s credits

How much are the Amex Platinum Card for Morgan Stanley’s credits worth?

$3,024 a year at face value across 12 credits, against a $895 annual fee. Covering the fee on credits alone takes 30% of that. $1,955 arrives in monthly, quarterly or half-year chunks that expire unused. What you reach depends on your own charges; Worth My Fee scores each credit against 12 months of real transactions.

Which Amex Platinum Card for Morgan Stanley credits expire every month?

Digital Entertainment Credit ($25 a month, $300 a year), Uber Cash ($15 a month, $200 a year) and Walmart+ Membership Credit ($13 a month, $155 a year). Each month’s amount is lost if unused, so the annual figure assumes a matching charge in all twelve months.

Does the Amex Platinum Card for Morgan Stanley’s Hotel Credit (FHR / The Hotel Collection) honor hotel elite status?

Generally yes. It books through a prepaid portal, but the issuer states these bookings are transmitted as qualifying rates, so points and elite recognition are usually earned. Reports of real-world honoring are mixed; confirm with the chain before relying on it.

Which Amex Platinum Card for Morgan Stanley credits require enrollment or activation?

Hotel Credit (FHR / The Hotel Collection), Resy Dining Credit, Digital Entertainment Credit, Uber Cash, Uber One Membership Credit, Airline Fee Credit, lululemon Credit, Oura Ring Credit, Equinox Credit and Walmart+ Membership Credit. They pay nothing until switched on; the issuer’s own statement-credit postings show whether that has happened.
