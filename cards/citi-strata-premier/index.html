Is the Citi Strata Premier worth its $95 annual fee?

Short answer: the Citi Strata Premier’s $95 fee is covered by one credit alone, Citi Travel Hotel Benefit ($100), if you actually use it, and Citi Travel Hotel Benefit voids hotel elite status on the stays it pays for. The catch is where it pays: $100 of the $100 advertised only works through Citi Travel, which voids hotel elite status on those stays.

Traps and friction

Where the Citi Strata Premier’s credits leak.

Portal hotel bookings void elite-status recognition. Citi Travel Hotel Benefit ($100 a year) pays only on prepaid bookings through Citi Travel, which makes the portal, not the hotel, the merchant of record. Major hotel programs treat these as third-party bookings: elite benefits are not honored at check-in (upgrades, breakfast, late checkout), and the stay earns no points. If you hold hotel status, redeeming this credit typically costs more per stay than the credit returns. If you are actively requalifying for status, every stay routed through the portal to capture this credit is a night that doesn’t count — and a shortfall at year-end can cost an entire tier, worth far more than the credit.Program terms: marriott.com, hilton.com, ihg.com, world.hyatt.com.

Questions

Citi Strata Premier credits, answered plainly.

How much are the Citi Strata Premier’s credits worth?

$100 a year at face value across 1 credit, against a $95 annual fee. Covering the fee on credits alone takes 95% of that. What you reach depends on your own charges; Worth My Fee scores each credit against 12 months of real transactions.

Does the Citi Strata Premier’s Citi Travel Hotel Benefit honor hotel elite status?

No. It pays only on prepaid bookings through Citi Travel, which makes the portal the merchant of record. Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt and IHG treat that as a third-party stay: no elite benefits at check-in, no points, and no qualifying nights toward status. If you hold hotel status, the credit typically costs more per stay than it returns.

What is the downgrade option for the Citi Strata Premier?

the Citi Strata ($0 a year) and the Citi Double Cash ($0 a year). Worth My Fee scores each on your same year of charges: if a cheaper card clears its fee the verdict is downgrade, and if none does the verdict is cancel.

What does the Citi Strata Premier earn on spending?

10× on hotels, car rentals & attractions via citi travel, 3× on air travel & other hotel purchases and 3× on restaurants, and 1× on everything else. Worth My Fee values Citi ThankYou at 1.5¢ per point, and counts only the premium over a no-fee 2% cash-back card toward the fee.
