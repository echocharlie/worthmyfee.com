Is the Hilton Honors American Express Business Card worth its $195 annual fee?

Short answer: the Hilton Honors Amex Business Card’s $195 fee is covered by one credit alone, Quarterly Hilton Credit ($240), if you actually use it. The catch is cadence: $240 of the $240 advertised arrives in monthly, quarterly or half-year chunks that expire unused.

The arithmetic

$240 advertised. $195 to cover.

To cover the $195 fee on credits alone you need to reach $195 of the $240 advertised, which is 82% of the headline number. That sounds easy. Whether it is depends on how the credits are shaped.

Of the $240: $240 arrives in monthly, quarterly or half-year chunks that do not roll over, so that credit needs matching spend on schedule, every period, or the money is gone.

None of this says what you will reach. That takes your own charges: the audit matches every credit against 12 months of real transactions, counts what actually landed on the card toward the fee, and shows the rest as upside.
