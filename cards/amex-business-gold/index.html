Is the American Express Business Gold worth its $375 annual fee?

Short answer: the Amex Business Gold’s $375 fee is covered by two credits alone, ChatGPT Business Credit ($300) and Flexible Business Credit (FedEx / Grubhub / Office Supply) ($240), if you actually use them. The catch is cadence: $395 of the $845 advertised arrives in monthly, quarterly or half-year chunks that expire unused.

What it takes to cover the $375 fee

To cover the $375 fee on credits alone you need to reach $375 of the $845 advertised, which is 45% of the headline number. How hard that is depends on when and where each credit pays.

Of the $845: $395 arrives in monthly, quarterly or half-year chunks that do not roll over, so those two credits need matching spend on schedule, every period, or the money is gone; $845 pays nothing until you enroll or activate it.

What you actually reach depends on your own charges: the audit matches every credit against 12 months of real transactions, counts what actually landed on the card toward the fee, and shows the rest as upside.

What makes the Amex Business Gold’s credits hard to use

  • Monthly credits. Flexible Business Credit (FedEx / Grubhub / Office Supply) and Walmart+ Membership Credit: $395 a year, but only if the matching charge lands every month. Five orders in a year on a monthly credit pays out for five months.
  • Enrollment required. Flexible Business Credit (FedEx / Grubhub / Office Supply), Walmart+ Membership Credit, Squarespace Credit and ChatGPT Business Credit pay nothing until enrolled or activated. The audit checks your statement for the issuer’s own credit postings, so an unenrolled credit shows up as exactly that.

Questions about the Amex Business Gold’s credits

How much are the Amex Business Gold’s credits worth?

$845 a year at face value across four credits, against a $375 annual fee. Covering the fee on credits alone takes 45% of that. $395 arrives in monthly, quarterly or half-year chunks that expire unused. What you reach depends on your own charges; Worth My Fee scores each credit against 12 months of real transactions.

Which Amex Business Gold credits expire every month?

Flexible Business Credit (FedEx / Grubhub / Office Supply) ($20 a month, $240 a year) and Walmart+ Membership Credit ($13 a month, $155 a year). Each month’s amount is lost if unused, so the annual figure assumes a matching charge in all twelve months.

Which Amex Business Gold credits require enrollment or activation?

Flexible Business Credit (FedEx / Grubhub / Office Supply), Walmart+ Membership Credit, Squarespace Credit and ChatGPT Business Credit. They pay nothing until switched on; the issuer’s own statement-credit postings show whether that has happened.

What is the downgrade option for the Amex Business Gold?

The American Express Business Green Rewards ($95 a year). Worth My Fee scores it on your same year of charges: if a cheaper card clears its fee the verdict is downgrade, and if none does the verdict is cancel.

What does the Amex Business Gold earn on spending?

4× on U.S. media purchases for advertising (flexible top-2), 4× on U.S. gas stations (flexible top-2) and 4× on U.S. restaurants (flexible top-2), and 1× on everything else. Worth My Fee values Amex Membership Rewards at 1.5¢ per point, and counts only the premium over a no-fee 2% cash-back card toward the fee.
