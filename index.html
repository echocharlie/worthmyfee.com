The part nobody else does

Some credits cost more than they give.

Every card app can remind you to use a credit. None of them ask whether you should.

The classic: a $500 hotel credit that only works through the card's own travel portal. Book through the portal, and the hotel treats it as a third-party prepaid stay — your elite status isn't honored, your night doesn't count toward requalification, your points don't post. The credit pays you $500 and quietly takes back more.

Worth My Fee models your loyalty programs alongside your card benefits, so every verdict is net of what a redemption would cost you elsewhere.

Trap detected: Using your card's $500 portal hotel credit would void elite-status recognition on the stays you booked with it — estimated to cost you more in lost upgrades, points, and qualifying nights than the credit returns.

Verdict: skip this credit; it's part of why this card fails its fee test.
