Airline and flight credits: every card that has one.

Flight credits pay against airfare or an airline’s own wallet, so they are reachable for anyone who flies that airline at all. The fine print is the clock: half-year deposits that expire, calendar-year windows, and credits that require the ticket to be bought with the card.
