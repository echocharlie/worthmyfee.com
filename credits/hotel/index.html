Hotel credits and free-night awards: every card that has one.

Direct hotel credits pay on stays booked with the chain itself, so elite status and points are preserved. Free-night awards are the opposite of a statement credit: capped by point value, sometimes gated on prior spend or a qualifying stay, and worth nothing in a year you do not use them.
