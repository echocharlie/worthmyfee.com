Dining and delivery credits: every card that has one.

Resy, DoorDash, Grubhub and restaurant credits pay on charges you may already make, which is why they score well in audits. The traps are cadence and channel: monthly promos that need separate orders each month, DashPass enrollment, and Resy credits that require a Resy-listed restaurant.
