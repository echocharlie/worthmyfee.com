Portal hotel credits: every card that has one.

These credits pay only on prepaid bookings made through the card issuer’s travel portal, which makes the portal, not the hotel, the merchant of record. Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt and IHG treat that as a third-party stay: no elite benefits at check-in, no points, no qualifying nights. If you hold hotel status, most of these are worth less than zero per stay. The two luxury programs that transmit bookings as qualifying rates are marked.
